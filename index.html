About Me

I am a Game Developer with over two years of experience building browser games using modern web technologies such as Cocos and PixiJS. I enjoy creating high-performance web games that run smoothly even on low-end devices while keeping the gameplay responsive and engaging.

In my professional work, I mainly use Cocos Creator to design scalable game architectures, write clean and maintainable code, and optimize rendering and asset workflows for browser environments.I also have experience working with other game frameworks and engines such as PixiJS, Phaser, and Unity.

Beyond gameplay development, I also enjoy building reusable systems and small utility tools that make development faster and improve the workflow for the whole team. I am always interested in solving technical challenges, improving game performance, and learning new tools and engines.
